## Overview

**Title:** Haikyu!! (ハイキュー!!)
**Genre:** Sports, Comedy, Drama
**Based on:** Manga by Haruichi Furudate
**Studio:** Production I.G

> "Humans can't fly, but they never stop trying."

## Main Characters

1. *Shoyo Hinata*: energetic middle blocker who is small but jumps very high
2. *Tobio Kageyama*: genius setter, known as the "King of the Court"
3. *Kei Tsukishima*: tall and sharp-minded middle blocker
4. *Daichi Sawamura*: calm and reliable team captain
